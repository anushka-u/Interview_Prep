You are given a 9-digit string containing every digit from 1 to 9 exactly once.

You can delete digits from the string, but:
- You cannot change the order of remaining digits.
- You cannot delete digits when only two digits remain.
- Your goal is to obtain a prime number.
If impossible, print -1. Otherwise, print any valid prime number.
Example
Input:
123456789

Output:
167


Here, we delete 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9 and retain 167.

Since 167 is prime, the answer is valid.
