Sieve of Eratosthenes — Easy Explanation
Think of it as a process of eliminating non-prime numbers.
1. First, assume all numbers from 2 to N are prime.
2. Pick a number that is still marked prime and cross out all its multiples because they cannot be prime.
3. Start crossing out from i * i, because smaller multiples have already been crossed out.
4. Repeat this process until √N.
5. Whatever numbers remain uncrossed are prime numbers.
Remember it like this:

Assume → Pick → Eliminate multiples → Repeat → Collect primes.

Example: For N = 20, you eliminate multiples of 2, 3 and 5. The remaining primes are:

2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19

Complexity:
- Time: O(N log log N)
- Space: O(N)
One-line revision: Instead of checking every number individually for primality, Sieve eliminates multiples of prime numbers to find all primes efficiently.
