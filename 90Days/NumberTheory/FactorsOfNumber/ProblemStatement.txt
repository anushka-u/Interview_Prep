Problem: Find All Factors of a Number
Easy
 Number Theory


Problem Statement:

Given a positive integer n, find and print all the factors (divisors) of that number in ascending order.

A factor of a number is an integer that divides the given number completely, leaving a remainder of zero.

Mathematically:
\[
n \bmod i = 0
\]

where i is a factor of n.
Examples
Example 1:
Input: n = 12
Output: 1 2 3 4 6 12

Explanation:
12 is divisible by 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 12.


Example 2:
Input: n = 20
Output: 1 2 4 5 10 20


Example 3:
Input: n = 7
Output: 1 7


Constraints
1 <= n <= 10^9
